focus pin state machine

Question 1 — does the pin / touch model behave the way it reads on paper? Two halves that never meet in one place — hooks/focus.sh decides what gets stored, sline/focus.go decides what gets shown — are both ported here so you can drive them together.
Question 2 (DOT-81) — is a stale Linear status tolerable in the line? Status is fetched by the hook and cached, never fetched at render. So the line can be wrong. The panel below lets you move a ticket in "Linear" behind sline's back and watch what happens.

⚠️ PROTOTYPE — throwaway. A hand port of the real logic, not the real code. Nothing here is imported by sline or the hook. If the two ever disagree, the shell and the Go win.
